ARCHITECTURE & ETHOS

Why Open Innovation Matters for AstroCast

1. Open-Weight Tabular AI (TabPFN)

Unlike chat-based LLMs that hallucinate atmospheric data, AstroCast uses open-weight tabular foundation models trained specifically on tabular meteorological features (dew point depression, micro-turbulent shear, cloud stratification).

2. Zero-Cloud Offline Inference

True outdoor stargazers hike miles away from cellular signal. AstroCast includes an autonomous offline microclimate simulation and local GBDT inference engine that runs completely disconnected on any laptop.

3. Screen Minimization by Design

Closed commercial apps maximize screen engagement with ads and infinite scroll. AstroCast does the opposite: it gives you one tabular score, one 3-item celestial checklist, and actively prompts you to lock the screen.
